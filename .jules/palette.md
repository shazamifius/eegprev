## 2026-01-06 - Loading States on Traditional Forms
**Learning:** Adding visual loading states (spinners) to traditional synchronous form submissions is a quick win. It provides immediate feedback even before the page reload happens, reducing "rage clicks".
**Action:** Apply `js-loading-form` pattern to other critical actions in the app.
